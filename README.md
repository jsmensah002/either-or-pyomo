A chemical plant makes two products, P and Q (tonnes/day), and must choose exactly one of two reactor types.

Always applies (either reactor):
- Feed: 1 t of feed per t of product, and only 60 t/day is available.
- Market demand: P is capped at 35 t/day. Q has unlimited demand.

Reactor 1 (CSTR):
- Profit: $50 per t of P, $40 per t of Q
- Steam: 2 t per t of P, 1 t per t of Q. 90 t/day available.

Reactor 2 (PFR):
- Profit: $70 per t of P, $40 per t of Q
- Steam: 3 t per t of P, 1 t per t of Q. 80 t/day available.

Goal: maximize profit, choosing one reactor.
