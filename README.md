A chemical plant produces ethanol (E), acetone (A) and butanol (B), in tonnes/day, and must choose exactly one of three separation units.

Always applies (whichever unit is chosen):
- Total feed: E + A + B can't exceed 100 t/day.
- Market demand: B is capped at 30 t/day.
- Contract: at least 10 t/day of E must be produced.

Distillation column:
- Profit: $60 per t of E, $45 per t of A, $80 per t of B
- Energy use: 3, 2 and 4 GJ per t of E, A and B. 250 GJ/day available.
- Fixed operating cost: $500/day

Membrane unit:
- Profit: $55 per t of E, $50 per t of A, $70 per t of B
- Energy use: 1, 1.5 and 2 GJ per t of E, A and B. 150 GJ/day available.
- Fixed operating cost: $300/day

Adsorption unit:
- Profit: $65 per t of E, $40 per t of A, $75 per t of B
- Energy use: 2, 2 and 3 GJ per t of E, A and B. 200 GJ/day available.
- Fixed operating cost: $400/day

Goal: maximize daily profit
